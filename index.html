SOHELA is a Paris-based DJ who has been mixing since childhood, raised inside a world of sound he never left. Rooted in tech house and minimal, his sets are built around groove and depth, letting tension climb before it breaks rather than chasing the easy drop.

Precise almost to a fault, he treats every set as one thing to get right: how it makes the room feel. Energetic but composed, equally at home on Paris club floors and in Tunis.
